### Strategic Objectives Enabled by This Lean Round:
1. **Captive Concierge Validation**: Execute initial 10–15 batch orders directly inside **Marks Engineering and Co.** and 2–3 trusted neighbor workshops in Chakan with zero software build expenses.
2. **Local Machine Shop Vetting**: Conduct 40-point physical audits across 15–20 precision workshops in Bhosari, Talwade, and Chakan GIDC.
3. **Dedicated Field Metrology**: Hire 1 full-time Lead Metrology QA Engineer equipped with digital verniers, micrometers, and bore gauges to inspect parts in-situ before dispatch.
4. **Pay-Per-Test Lab Network**: Partner with accredited NABL metallurgy testing labs on an on-demand basis (₹500–₹1,500/test) only when an order requires CMM or spectrometry.
5. **Path to Institutional Seed**: Reach a ₹75 Lakhs+/month GMV run-rate with 12% take-rates and $< 1\%$ scrap rate, unlocking a $1.5M institutional Seed Round at Month 18.

## 5. Granular Unit Economics: Standard Batch Order Case Study

Below is the unit economics model for a typical **₹5,00,000 CNC Precision Machined Component Batch** (e.g., 250 units of SS 316L Valve Spindles with ground bearing journals):

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    TRANSACTION UNIT ECONOMICS                                      |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  1. Buyer Purchase Order Value (Milestone Terms: 30% Adv / 40% Insp / 30% Delivery)  ₹5,00,000     |
|  2. Vendor Cost of Goods Sold (COGS Payout):                                     - ₹4,40,000       |
|     - Tranche 1 (Gate 1 - Certified SS316L Raw Material MTC):      ₹1,35,000                       |
|     - Tranche 2 (Gate 2/3 - 4-Axis CNC Spindle & Machining):       ₹1,75,000                       |
|     - Tranche 3 (Gate 4 - Final Inspection Sign-off & Inward):     ₹1,30,000                       |
|  3. Gross Platform Take-Rate Revenue (12.0%)                                         ₹60,000       |
|  4. Direct Cost of Quality (COQ):                                                -   ₹9,000        |
|     - On-Demand NABL Metallurgy Spectrometry (OES PMI Test):        ₹2,500                         |
|     - Quality Inspector Field Travel & Digital Inspection Time:     ₹3,500                         |
|     - GPS Transport e-Way Bill Admin & Transit Insurance:           ₹3,000                         |
|  5. Net Contribution Margin (85.0% of Revenue)                                       ₹51,000       |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Capital Efficiency Advantages:
* **Back-to-Back Milestone Alignment**: Buyer advance funds vendor raw material purchase. RKISPro carries **zero working capital inventory debt**.
* **High Net Contribution Margin (85.0%)**: No fixed lab overhead. Metrology testing is outsourced on a per-test basis (₹500–₹1,500/test) only when required by buyer specifications.
* **Repeat Buyer Velocity**: Industrial manufacturers order components on monthly or quarterly recurring schedules. Once a supplier is qualified, repeat customer acquisition cost is near zero.

## 7. Key Financial Controls & Risk Safeguards

1. **Back-to-Back Milestone Settlement**: Direct uncollateralized vendor advances are strictly prohibited. Funds are disbursed in 30/40/30 tranches linked directly to physical quality sign-offs.
2. **Marks Engineering & Co. Anchor Subsidy**: Co-founder Rupesh Tiwari’s operational workshop provides immediate physical machining capacity and welding bays, saving ₹15L–₹20L in third-party tooling and setup expenses during the concierge pilot phase.
3. **Statutory Tax Alignment**: Automated SAC 9988 GST e-invoicing guarantees input tax credit (ITC) flow, protecting buyers and RKISPro from tax friction.
