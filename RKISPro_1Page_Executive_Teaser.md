### Executive Overview
**RKISPro** is a lean, managed B2B industrial manufacturing marketplace replacing unvetted directory classifieds (e.g., IndiaMART) with an engineering-governed, contractually guaranteed production network. Instead of building expensive software upfront, RKISPro leverages the founders' generational manufacturing roots and captive workshop capacity (**Marks Engineering and Co.**) to deliver precision metal fabrication with manual drawing triage, rigorous 4-gate shop-floor quality inspection, and structured commercial milestone terms.

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    THE LEAN MANAGED VALUE CHAIN                                    |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  [ Industrial Buyer ]      ──>  [ 4-Gate Quality & Drawing Triage ]  ──>  [ Vetted Machine Shops ]  |
|   - 2D/3D Drawing RFQ Upload     - 15-Min Human DFM & Quote Review         - Marks Engineering Anchor
|   - Milestone PO Terms           - On-Demand NABL Metrology Tests          - Audited Chakan MSMEs   |
|   - Single Contractual Seller    - Pre-Dispatch Sign-off & Inspection      - Timely Milestone Payout|
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Market Sizing & Target Corridors

* **Total Addressable Market (TAM) — $120 Billion**: Annual Indian contract manufacturing, CNC machining, structural fabrication, and industrial plant breakdown maintenance.
* **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) — $24 Billion**: High-precision contract machining & fabricated equipment across automotive, defense, aerospace, pharma, and cement.
* **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) — $25 Million**: Initial 24–36 month focus on Pune-Chakan industrial cluster (Bhosari, Talwade, Chakan GIDC).

### What Has Been Built to Date (Ideation Phase Assets)

1. **Interactive WebGL 3D CAD Metrology Inspector**: Live Three.js browser inspector with 360° orbit drag, alloy shaders (SS316, Al6061, Brass), wireframe, and GD&T datum callouts.
2. **Instant Machining Cost Estimator**: Parametric cost model calculating material weight, CNC spindle hours, and secondary heat/plating treatments.
3. **Enterprise Marketplace UI**: 48-route responsive Next.js web application with multi-tenant buyer/vendor authentication and project tracking.
4. **Validated Demand & Supply Network**: 35+ discovery interviews with plant procurement heads; 8 preliminary partner machine shops pre-vetted in Chakan.

### 3-Stage Pragmatic Execution Roadmap

* **Stage 1 (Months 1–3) — Concierge Pilot (Zero Software Capex)**: Fulfill first 10–15 orders directly via Marks Engineering & Co. and 2–3 trusted neighbor shops. Founders personally triage drawings and conduct manual 4-gate inspection. Target: ₹24L–₹35L GMV.
* **Stage 2 (Months 4–9) — Local Micro-Network (Lean Ask Deployment)**: Onboard 8–12 vetted partner shops in Bhosari/Chakan. Hire 1 full-time Field Quality Inspector. Partner with local NABL labs on a pay-per-test basis (₹500–₹1,500/test). Deliver 35–45 commercial batches. Target: ₹1.7 Cr cumulative GMV.
* **Stage 3 (Months 10–18) — Digital Network Scale**: Launch simple web portal for automated RFQ routing, expand to 25+ audited workshops, reach ₹75L+/mo run rate $\rightarrow$ Command $1.5M Institutional Seed round.
